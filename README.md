## 📖 What is Academy Notify?

**Academy Notify** is an **offline-first desktop application** built for the administrators and owners of **private tutoring academies and learning centers**. It centralises everything you need to run your academy: student records, teacher assignments, course scheduling, revision session tracking, and instant messaging — all from a single, clean interface.

Your data stays on your own computer, secured and private. No cloud. No server. No subscription.

---

## 👥 Who Is It For?

| Role | How they benefit |
|------|-----------------|
| **Academy Owner / Admin** | Full control over students, teachers, courses, and messaging from one screen |
| **Secretary / Receptionist** | Quickly enroll students, view today's schedule, and send reminders |
| **Teachers** (managed, not users) | Their information, subjects, and schedules are tracked by the admin |

> This app is designed for **small to medium private academies** — especially those in Arabic-speaking regions — that teach groups or individual students and need to communicate efficiently with parents and students via Telegram or WhatsApp.

---

## 🎯 What Can It Do?

### 👤 Student Management
- Add, edit, and delete student records (name, phone, parent contact, level, enrollment)
- Enroll students in one or multiple courses
- View per-student messaging history
- **Self-registration via Telegram** — students can register themselves through your bot

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Management
- Maintain a full teacher roster with contact info and subjects
- Assign teachers to courses
- View each teacher's schedule at a glance

### 📚 Course Management
- Create **group** or **private (1-on-1)** courses
- Assign teachers, levels, subjects, and rooms
- Filter courses by level, teacher, day, or room
- Print course schedules per course or for the entire filtered list
- Select individual courses with checkboxes and print a custom batch schedule

### 🗓️ Weekly Schedule
- Visual weekly timetable with room-by-room grid view
- A4 table print or weekly grid print
- Filter and print only what you need (by day, teacher, course, or level)

### 📝 Revision Sessions
- Create one-off revision or exam-prep sessions per course
- Track whether notifications have been sent
- Auto-schedule reminders to enrolled students

### 📤 Send Messages
- Broadcast to **all students** or **by course / by level**
- Send to a **single student** directly
- Choose Telegram, WhatsApp, or both simultaneously
- Use **smart templates** with `{{name}}`, `{{course}}`, `{{date}}` placeholders
- Built-in bilingual templates (Arabic & English ready)

### 💬 Chat (Telegram)
- View incoming messages from students directly in the app
- Reply from the admin panel

### 📊 Message Logs
- Full delivery history with status (sent / failed)
- Filter logs by date, student, or course

### 🖨️ Advanced Printing
- Print in **A4** (portrait / landscape) or **thermal receipt** (80mm / 58mm) format
- Smart print preview before sending to printer
- Print filtered-only data — the printout always matches exactly what you see on screen

### 🌐 Bilingual Interface
- Fully translated in **Arabic (RTL)** and **English (LTR)**
- Switch language at any time from Settings

## ⚙️ First-Time Setup

When you open the app for the first time:

1. **Go to Settings** (bottom of the sidebar)
2. Enter your **Academy Name**, phone, and address — these appear on printed documents
3. Set up at least one messaging channel (Telegram or WhatsApp) — see below
4. Start adding your **teachers**, then **courses**, then **students**

---

## 📱 Setting Up Telegram Notifications

1. Open Telegram and search for **@BotFather**
2. Send `/newbot` and follow the instructions
3. Copy the **bot token** (e.g. `7812345678:AAHdqTcvCHi...`)
4. In the app → **Settings** → paste the token under **"Telegram Bot Token"**
5. Click **Test Connection** — you should see a success message
6. Share your bot link with students: `https://t.me/YourBotName`

> Students must press **START** on the bot once before you can message them.

---

## 📲 Setting Up WhatsApp Notifications

1. Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com) and create a **Meta Business App**
2. Add the **WhatsApp** product
3. Get your **Phone Number ID** and **Access Token**
4. In the app → **Settings** → fill in both fields under **"WhatsApp Business API"**
5. Click **Test Connection**

> Meta offers a messaging quota per month. Check their pricing page for details.

---

## 🖨️ How to Use the Print Feature

1. Navigate to any section (Courses, Schedule, Students, etc.)
2. Use the **filters** (level, teacher, day, room, search) to narrow down the data
3. Click the **🖨️ Print** button in the top bar
4. A **print preview** opens — choose:
   - **Paper format:** A4 table, A4 weekly grid, or thermal receipt
   - **Scope:** Print only selected rows, or all filtered results
   - **Orientation:** Portrait or landscape
5. Click **Print** to send to your printer or save as PDF

> The print output always matches exactly what the filter shows — nothing extra is ever included.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**App won't open:**
- Restart the application
- Contact support if the issue persists

**Messages not sending:**
- Double-check your bot token or WhatsApp credentials in Settings
- Use the **"Test Connection"** button to verify
- Ensure your device has internet access

**Students aren't receiving messages:**
- Telegram: make sure the student pressed **START** on your bot
- WhatsApp: the number must match the format used when registering
